# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Senior professionals, executives, and retirees in Nigeria (and the diaspora) who are weighing or actively planning the move from a corporate career into business ownership. They arrive with real capital, real risk aversion, and no patience for generic startup content aimed at 25-year-olds. They come to this site to size up whether Dr. Olalere's judgment is worth trusting, then to act: register for the Business Transition Masterclass, request the Corporate-to-Business Transition Checklist, or enquire about private advisory or a speaking engagement.

## Product Purpose

A personal-brand and lead-generation site for Dr. (Mrs.) Omowunmi Olalere, Founder & CEO of Numero Group. It exists to establish her authority (real career history, real companies, real credentials) and convert that authority into three concrete actions: masterclass seats, checklist opt-ins, and advisory/speaking enquiries. Success looks like a visitor who arrived skeptical leaving with one of those three actions taken, not just "impressed."

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, editorial, direct. The voice of someone who has actually done the thing she's advising on, not a coach reciting frameworks. Confident without hype: facts, real numbers where they exist, honest bracketed placeholders where they don't (never fabricated). Sharp corners over rounded pills, serif display type over generic geometric sans, black/gold/green (from her real logo) over a generic SaaS palette.

## Anti-references

Generic AI-slop / startup-landing-page tropes: gradient text, glassmorphism-as-decoration, pill-everything, side-stripe accent borders, identical 3-card grids, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section, numbered 01/02/03 scaffolding where there's no real sequence, fabricated stats/testimonials/scarcity meters, aphoristic "serious statement, then punchy negation" copy cadence, marketing buzzwords (streamline, empower, leverage, unlock).

## Design Principles

- Real content only. Unknown facts render as honest bracketed placeholders (`[Venue]`, `[Price]`), never invented.
- Earn authority through specifics (real companies, real dates, real credentials), not through generic confidence language.
- Every page has one job (learn her story, register, get the checklist, read an article) and one dominant CTA toward it.
- Asymmetric, editorial layouts over equal-card grids; hierarchy through scale and weight, not decoration.
- Consistency of brand chrome (nav, footer, type, color, corner radius) across every page, even where a page's own mood shifts (e.g. the masterclass page's darker hero).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG level has been set; target AA contrast (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text/UI) as the working bar. Motion is currently limited to scroll-reveal fades and hover transitions; add `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks wherever motion is introduced or extended.
