She founded Numero Group and the My Business and I platform on one conviction: the professionals who have earned the most are often given the least guidance at the moment it matters most, the point of transition.

  • A clear assessment of which business model fits your experience, capital, and lifestyle, not what's trendy.

How the day is structured
9:00
Arrival, coffee and introductions
Meet your cohort, professionals and executives at the same crossroads.
9:30
Session 1. The transition reality check
What changes when you leave corporate, what your experience is worth, and the mistakes first-time owners make with savings.
11:00
Session 2. Finding your business fit
A live working session mapping your skills, network, capital, and risk tolerance to two or three directions that genuinely fit.
13:00
Networking lunch
Included. Facilitated peer conversations between attendees.
14:00
Session 3. Capital, risk, and protection
How to invest without risking your retirement security. Structuring entry capital. What never to do with a pension or lump sum.
15:30
Session 4. Your 90-day entry plan
You leave with a written, working plan for your first 90 days in business.
16:45
Open Q&A with Dr. Olalere
Your specific questions. Real, direct answers.
17:30
Closing and next steps
Your accountability plan for the 48 hours after you leave the room.
