About Dr. Olalere

She has done the very thing she now guides others through.

Dr. (Mrs.) Omowunmi Olalere · Founder & CEO, Numero Group · Business Advisor & Educator

A visionary executive whose work sits at the meeting point of real estate, agriculture, and enterprise development, guided by a deep interest in the food and housing industries and a comprehensive understanding of business management and the wider economic balances that shape it.

From banking to building

Her career began at ASO Savings and Loans Plc, where she rose to Team Leader, Small and Medium Business Banking, before joining the then Access Homes and Mortgages in 2010 as Team Lead, Credit and Mortgages. Absorbed into Access Bank Plc in 2011, she advanced to Relationship Manager and earned the bank's 2015 Best Relationship Manager award.

After more than a decade in banking, she stepped away in 2017. Within a year, she had established the Numero Group of Companies, a conglomerate registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission. She has done the very thing she now guides others through: the move from a career built inside someone else's structure to a business built on her own terms.

The Numero Group

The conglomerate she built comprises four registered companies, each extending her interest in real estate, agriculture, and enterprise into its own focused operation.

Real EstateNumero Homes Global Resources Ltd
AgricultureNumero Farms Ltd
FacilitiesNumero Facilities and Maintenance Ltd
CommerceDozen Price Enterprises Ltd

Extending the value chain

Driven to extend her value chain to the mass affluent, she went on to found the Numero Cooperative Multipurpose Society, which stimulates savings among members and supports their housing needs, and A202 Academy Ltd, an institute that trains aspiring entrepreneurs to discover their potential and launch their own businesses.

Beyond business

Her commitment to sport led to her election as Chairperson of the Southwest Gymnastics Federation of Nigeria and her appointment as a National Board Member of the Gymnastics Federation of Nigeria. To nurture young sporting talent, she founded The Omowunmi Olalere Foundation.

A leader of integrity

A generous and humble leader of unwavering integrity, Dr. (Mrs.) Omowunmi Olalere is intelligent, disciplined, and devoted to hard work, attributes that have allowed her to merge her many passions into enterprises that now command a substantial market share across the board.
